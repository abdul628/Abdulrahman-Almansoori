About

Operations, data, and execution — at the intersection.

I lead Business Excellence and Analytics at National Mineral Water Company, an FMCG producer and distributor in Oman. My work turns raw ERP and sales outputs into structured dashboards, KPIs, and reporting workflows that the General Manager and every department actually use to make decisions.

Alongside the BI stack, I build LLM-powered tools that sit on top of business data — automating document and text processing, embedding GPT-driven assistance into dashboards, and using Python with AI APIs to compress the work that used to need a person.

My background is Industrial Engineering at Sultan Qaboos University (ABET-accredited; graduated September 2024 with Honors, GPA 3.66, first in class). I think in systems: process, throughput, variation, root cause. The dashboards are the surface — the real work is structuring how a company sees itself.
